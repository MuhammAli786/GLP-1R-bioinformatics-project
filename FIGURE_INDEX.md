## Notes on matching

- **Fig01, Fig02B, Fig06B, Fig07, Fig08D, Fig09, Fig10** were confirmed by exact MD5 hash match against the images embedded in the deck.
- The remaining figures were re-encoded by PowerPoint on insertion, so they were matched by figure **title text + exact pixel dimensions** against candidate source files (e.g. Fig08A–C at 4241×4151, Fig05 at 2335×1508, Fig02 at 4682×876).
- Two deck panels were embedded as EMF vector graphics (a per-accession KEGG ORA dot plot and the inflammatory Cnet); the corresponding raster/PDF source files are provided here in their place.
- The deck's Fig 3 and Fig 4 both describe per-accession KEGG ORA dot plots; they are represented here by the single cross-accession KEGG ORA dot plot (`Fig03-04_…`).
